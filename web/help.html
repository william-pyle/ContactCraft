ContactSolutions help

ContactSolutions keeps contacts and contact groups on your Windows computer. Each contact can have a picture and theme, and other applications can use the shared store.

What's in it

  • Every field Google Contacts, Outlook and vCard can hold, and your own fields for anything else.
  • A picture and a theme for each person: banner colour, pattern or picture, font, text size and colour, background colour or picture, message bubble colours and sounds.
  • Named contact groups with themes and any mix of saved contacts and number-only members.
  • Import from Google Contacts (vCard or Google CSV), Outlook (CSV) and iPhone (vCard); export to vCard, Google CSV or ContactSolutions's own file.
  • Shared with Craft apps whether ContactSolutions is open or not.

Getting started

  1. Download the installer (or the portable zip) from the latest release and run it.
  2. Click Import, and choose the file you exported from Google Contacts or Outlook.
  3. Pick a contact, then Edit to add details, or Theme to give them a look. Use New Group to collect contacts and phone numbers.

To look around without touching your contacts, start ContactSolutions.exe --demo.

Privacy

Your contacts never leave your computer. They're kept in your Windows profile (%APPDATA%\ContactSolutions), and only programs you run can open them. The only thing ContactSolutions does online is ask GitHub, when it starts, whether a newer version has been released; nothing about you or your contacts is sent, and you can turn it off in File › Options.
